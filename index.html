1. Advanced Sales & Deal Expressions

EXPRESSION 1 Negotiation Framing

Anchor the negotiation

기선 제압 / 협상 제안 기준점 설정

Definition: Establishing a strategic initial price or term that serves as the psychological benchmark for all subsequent counteroffers.

"By submitting our premium proposal first, we effectively anchored the negotiation at a higher price ceiling."
EXPRESSION 2 Risk Management

Risk exposure

위험 노출도 / 재무적 위험 부담

Definition: The quantified potential loss or operational liability a company faces in a commercial contract.

"Our liability caps are strictly designed to minimize our risk exposure during regional power grid outages."
EXPRESSION 3 Concession Tactics

Strategic concession

전략적 양보 (대가성 조건 완화)

Definition: A calculated compromise offered during talks to secure a higher-value commitment in return.

"We offered a 5% volume discount as a strategic concession in exchange for a mandatory three-year contract."
EXPRESSION 4 Account Expansion

Value-driven upsell

가치 기반 추가 판매 (추가 매출 창출)

Definition: Persuading an existing buyer to purchase upgraded capabilities by demonstrating incremental ROI.

"By highlighting predictive maintenance analytics, our team executed a successful value-driven upsell."
EXPRESSION 5 Pricing Model

Tiered pricing structure

단계별 요금 체계

Definition: A commercial model offering distinct feature sets or volume brackets at progressive price points.

"We implemented a tiered pricing structure to accommodate both regional buyers and enterprise groups."
EXPRESSION 6 Commercial Outcome

Mutual gain

상호 이익 / 공동 성과

Definition: A balanced negotiation agreement where both parties secure substantial commercial or strategic advantage.

"Structuring long-term maintenance service alongside the equipment deployment ensures true mutual gain."

2. Financial Verbs & Executive Descriptors

• Amortize (초기 비용을) 분할 감가상각하다

"Clients can amortize the upfront infrastructure cost over a five-year service term."

• Operationalize (계획·전략을) 실무에 적용하다, 가동하다

"Our engineering team will operationalize the custom firmware upgrade immediately."

• Consolidate (계약·물량을) 통합하다

"By consolidating regional orders, you qualify for our enterprise pricing bracket."

• Recoup (투자금·비용을) 회수하다

"Buyers usually recoup their initial investment within nine operational months."

• Prohibitive (비용 등이 터무니없이 비싼)  |  • Non-negotiable (타협 불가능한 조건인)

3. Advanced Sales Prepositions

Subject to ~을 조건으로 하는, ~에 따라

"This customized pricing proposal is subject to executive board approval."

In tandem with ~와 병행하여, 결합하여

"We deliver specialized hardware in tandem with 24/7 cloud monitoring."

In lieu of ~ 대신에

"We can offer extended warranty terms in lieu of an immediate cash discount."

At a premium 할증 가격으로, 높은 가치로

"Expedited delivery lines are available at a premium during peak shipping months."
