2. Versatile High-Level Prepositions

In light of ~에 비추어 볼 때, ~를 고려하여

"In light of recent market fluctuations, we have re-evaluated our expansion timeline."

Notwithstanding ~에도 불구하고

"Notwithstanding severe supply chain bottlenecks, our team delivered the project ahead of schedule."

In alignment with ~와 발맞추어, ~에 부합하여

"Our strategic objectives are structured in alignment with global sustainability goals."

At the expense of ~를 희생하면서 / 손상시키면서

"We should never accelerate production velocity at the expense of product quality."

3. Strategic Reframing Sentence Patterns

PATTERN A: The Diplomatic Counter-Pivot

While [Current Constraint] presents challenges, [Strategic Action] allows us to [Target Advantage] without [Negative Side-effect].

"While tight budgets present challenges, pivoting our marketing focus allows us to capture higher-margin leads without increasing total spend."

PATTERN B: The Collaborative Compromise Frame

In alignment with [Shared Goal], meeting [Counterparty] halfway on [Point A] will pave the way for [Key Benefit] moving forward.

"In alignment with our long-term partnership, meeting the client halfway on warranty terms will pave the way for a multi-year master agreement."
